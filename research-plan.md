## 9. AW Execution Contract

`Work → Job → Task → Action → Outcome → Evidence`

Completion is judged by whether the planned research outcome can be evaluated with evidence, not merely whether an action succeeded.

## 10. Evidence Discipline

`Observation ≠ Interpretation ≠ Hypothesis ≠ Conclusion`

Verification itself is not automatically evidence. A verified observation/result becomes evidence when provenance and validity criteria are satisfied.

## 11. Revision

A thesis may be:

- strengthened
- weakened
- qualified
- split
- restricted in scope
- expanded in scope
- refuted
- replaced

Never overwrite historical claims. Record revisions chronologically.

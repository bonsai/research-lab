# Research Domains

Research domains are independent research spaces registered under the generic `research-lab` template.

## Domains

| ID | Repository | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| worldmodel | `bonsai/research-worldmodel` | World Models, views, embodiment, prediction |
| architecture | `bonsai/research-architecture` | Architecture, space, built environment |
| animation | `bonsai/research-animation` | Animation, motion, image, narrative |
| pic | `bonsai/research-pic` | PIC, hardware chips, embedded computation |

## Domain contract

Each domain repository owns its substantive research questions, corpus, experiments, evidence, and thesis history. `research-lab` owns the common research process and domain registry.

Canonical flow:

`RP → RQ → RW → TF → AW → Observation → Evidence → Evaluation → Thesis Revision → Next RQ`

Cross-domain relationships are allowed. A research question may depend on, generate, or transfer knowledge to another domain.
